RiskRunway Quote Parser

Chrome Extension Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the RiskRunway Quote Parser Chrome Extension (the "Extension"), published by RiskRunway. The Extension allows insurance agents to open a commercial insurance quote PDF in Chrome, click the extension icon, and have the relevant policy data parsed and displayed in a popup for easy copy-paste into their agency management system (AMS).

Our core principle: we process your PDF to do the job, then it's gone. We do not store, sell, or share the contents of your quote documents.

1. What the Extension Does

When you activate the Extension on a quote PDF open in Chrome:

  1. The Extension reads the content of the currently open PDF tab
  2. That content is transmitted securely (HTTPS/TLS) to the RiskRunway server
  3. Our AI service (Amazon Bedrock / Claude by Anthropic) extracts relevant policy fields
  4. The parsed data is returned and displayed in the Extension popup
  5. You copy the data you need into your AMS
  6. The PDF content is discarded immediately — nothing is stored on our servers

2. Information We Access

PDF Content

The Extension reads the text content of the active PDF tab solely to perform quote data extraction. This content is transmitted to our server for AI processing and is not retained after the response is returned to you.

No Other Data

The Extension does not access your browsing history, other tabs, cookies, passwords, or any data outside of the specific PDF you activate it on. It does not track your activity.

3. Data Transmission and Storage

  • All data transmitted between the Extension and our server is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • PDF content is sent to our AWS-hosted server in the United States for AI processing
  • Parsed results are returned to your browser and displayed in the popup
  • We do not store PDF content, parsed results, or any document data after processing is complete
  • No quote data is written to a database or retained on our servers

4. AI Processing

Quote data extraction is performed using Amazon Bedrock (Claude by Anthropic), hosted within our AWS infrastructure in the United States. Document content is used only for the purpose of field extraction and is not used to train AI models. AWS and Anthropic's data processing terms apply.

5. Permissions We Request

The Extension requests only the permissions necessary to function:

  • activeTab — to read the content of the PDF you have open when you click the extension icon
  • scripting — to extract text from the active PDF tab
  • storage — to remember minimal preferences such as your last used settings (no document content is stored)

We do not request broad host permissions, access to all sites, or any permission beyond what is needed to parse the active PDF.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only as required to operate the service:

  • Amazon Web Services — infrastructure hosting for processing
  • Anthropic / AWS Bedrock — AI extraction only, during processing, not retained
  • Law enforcement — only if required by law or valid legal process

7. Who Can Use This Extension

The RiskRunway Quote Parser is a business tool intended for use by licensed insurance professionals. It is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the Chrome Web Store listing and at the URL below. Continued use of the Extension after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
